## 1. Summary of Changes

### Network Plane Separation, SOGo Ingress Token, Gitea Secrets & `DOCKER-USER` WAN Egress (`CRIT-02`)
- **Modified** `docker-compose.yml`: assigned deterministic IPAM subnets (`172.28.0.0/24` through `172.28.7.0/24`), explicit bridge names (`br-ingress`, `br-internal`, `br-control`, `br-mail`, `br-secrets`, `br-ide`, etc.), and `gw_priority` ordering.
- **Modified** `docker/compose/compose.agents.yml`: detached `runner-hermes` from `brainsos-ingress-net` so no runner service shares the external ingress plane.
- **Modified** `docker/sogo/nginx.conf`, `docker/sogo/Dockerfile`, `docker/sogo/entrypoint.sh`, and `config/caddy/{Caddyfile,Caddyfile.appliance}`: enforced a verified `X-Caddy-Ingress-Token` header between Caddy and `sogo:20000` (fail-closed `403`, single-pass `awk` entrypoint substitution without infinite loops).
- **Modified** `docker/compose/compose.apps.yml`, `scripts/control/generate-env.sh`, `packages/brainsOS-appliance/scripts/firstboot.sh`, and `.env.example`: scoped `GITEA__security__REVERSE_PROXY_TRUSTED_PROXIES` strictly to Caddy `/32` endpoints and auto-generated cryptographically random `GITEA_SECRET_KEY` and `GITEA_INTERNAL_TOKEN` (removing static fallbacks).
- **Modified** `scripts/setup/imds-guard.sh`: added `BRAINSOS-EGRESS` / `BRAINSOS-EGRESS6` chains in `DOCKER-USER` restricting `brainsos-internal-net` WAN egress exclusively through `tool-egress-proxy:8082`.

### Strict TLS SNI Presence, Peer IP Validation & Tavily Scrubbing in `tool-egress-proxy` (`CRIT-03`, `MED-06`)
- **Modified** `config/egress/addons/github_auth.py`: implemented `_verify_tls_and_sni` requiring non-empty TLS `sni` matching both `flow.request.host` and `flow.request.pretty_host`; implemented `http_connect`, `server_connect`, and post-connect `flow.server_conn.peername` validation blocking RFC1918, loopback, link-local, CGNAT, IMDSv4/v6, ULA, and Docker-bridge destinations (`_is_forbidden_ip`); updated Tavily egress injection to transport `TAVILY_API_KEY` via `Authorization: Bearer` while scrubbing `api_key` from JSON request bodies so `mitmweb` retains no unredacted credentials.

### Externalized OpenBao Seal, Least-Privilege Token, Internal TLS & `code-server` Containment (`HIGH-03`)
- **Modified** `config/openbao/openbao.hcl`, `docker/compose/{compose.control.yml,compose.agents.yml,compose.ingress.yml}`, `config/caddy/{Caddyfile,Caddyfile.appliance}`, and `scripts/verify/verify-appliance-ingress.sh`: enabled OpenBao internal TLS (`tls_disable = 0`) with CA certificate mounts across Caddy, System API, and `tool-egress-proxy`.
- **Modified** `scripts/setup/setup-secrets.sh`, `packages/brainsOS-telemetry/brainsos_telemetry/openbao_client.py`, `scripts/control/reload-env.sh`, and `scripts/control/bootstrap-env.sh`: relocated OpenBao seal material outside `${BRAINSOS_DATA_DIR}` to `/etc/brainsos/openbao-seal.json` (`0600 root:root`, with gitignored host-only fallback for rootless local dev), provisioned least-privilege `system-api-kv-writer` / `oidc-sync` tokens, revoked the initial root token, and eliminated `docker exec -e` token/unseal argument passing.
- **Modified** `docker/compose/compose.apps.yml`, `docker/editor/entrypoint.sh`, `config/editor/{settings.json,brainsos.code-workspace}`, `config/templates/data-repo.gitignore`, `.gitignore`, and `scripts/setup/setup-git.sh`: masked `/data/control_plane` and `/data/backups` in `code-server` with read-only `tmpfs` mounts, removed `--disable-workspace-trust`, and ignored `control_plane/**`, `backups/**`, `*.key`, `*.pem`, and `.env*` in `data-repo.gitignore`.

### IMDS/Route 53 Reconciliation & Bridge-Scoped Host Listeners (`MED-02`)
- **Modified** `scripts/setup/imds-guard.sh` and `scripts/control/reload-env.sh`: reconciled `BRAINSOS-IMDS` (`#1`) and `BRAINSOS-EGRESS` (`#2`) ordering in `DOCKER-USER` (including IPv6 `--check` parity), allowed Caddy on IMDS solely when `BRAINSOS_TLS_MODE=route53`, and re-applied `imds-guard.sh` during `reload-env.sh`.
- **Modified** `packages/brainsOS-appliance/scripts/{harden-host.sh,firstboot.sh}`, `packages/brainsOS-agent/brainsos_agent/worker.py`, `scripts/control/start-control-plane.sh`, and `scripts/verify/{verify-editor.sh,verify-portal.sh}`: enforced bridge-scoped host listeners (`8010/tcp` strictly on `br-ingress`, `8000/tcp` strictly on `br-mail`), hardened UFW rule inspection against all-port/range/multiport bypasses, and hardened `_is_unspecified_host` / `_normalize_bind_host` against shorthand/octal/hex/IDNA unspecified forms.
- **Modified** `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `scripts/verify/verify-egress-token-injection.sh`: updated documentation for `OpenBaoEgressAddon`, `[INJECTED_OPENBAO_SECRET]`, `/etc/brainsos/openbao-seal.json`, and `8010`/`8000` port separation, and aligned `verify-egress-token-injection.sh` with non-destructive offline/safe verification.
