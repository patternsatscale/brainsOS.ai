## 1. Summary of Changes

### Default Egress Proxy Auth & `TOOL_EGRESS_PROXY_SECRET` Provisioning (`HIGH-2`)
- **Modified** `.env.example`, `scripts/control/generate-env.sh`, and `packages/brainsOS-appliance/scripts/firstboot.sh`:
  - Added `TOOL_EGRESS_REQUIRE_PROXY_AUTH=true` and `TOOL_EGRESS_PROXY_SECRET=` to `.env.example` (plus `# BRAINSOS_SANDBOX_SUBNET=172.28.8.0/24` and narrowed `AUTH_ALB_TRUSTED_CIDRS`).
  - Added `"TOOL_EGRESS_PROXY_SECRET": lambda: gen_hex(32)` (32 random bytes / 64 hex chars), `"tool_egress_proxy_secret"` placeholder detection, and `req_key` fallback generation to `scripts/control/generate-env.sh`.
  - Added `TOOL_EGRESS_PROXY_SECRET` dry-run logging, existing-`.env` placeholder backfill (preserving valid values on warm-standby EBS re-claims), and `APPLIANCE_OVERRIDES` (`TOOL_EGRESS_REQUIRE_PROXY_AUTH=true`, `TOOL_EGRESS_PROXY_SECRET=$(gen_secret 32)`) to `packages/brainsOS-appliance/scripts/firstboot.sh`.
- **Modified** `docker/compose/compose.agents.yml`:
  - Set `TOOL_EGRESS_REQUIRE_PROXY_AUTH=${TOOL_EGRESS_REQUIRE_PROXY_AUTH:-true}` on `tool-egress-proxy` so `EphemeralDockerRunnerClient` (`v2.` HMAC tokens) and `OpenBaoEgressAddon` enforce per-tenant proxy authentication by default.

### Fail-Closed GitHub Repo Allowlist, DNS `OSError` & Post-Handshake `server_connected` (`HIGH-2`, `INFO-2`)
- **Modified** `config/egress/addons/github_auth.py`:
  - Updated `is_blocked_destination` to default `resolver: Optional[Any] = None` (evaluating `socket.getaddrinfo` at call time) and fail closed (`return f"DNS resolution failed..."`) on `OSError` or empty DNS results.
  - Updated `_check_github_allowed_repos` to fail closed whenever `allowed_set` is `None` or empty, while honoring explicit `"*"` wildcard intersection only after backslash and path-traversal (`..`, `posixpath.normpath`) checks pass.
  - Implemented `server_connected(self, data)` immediately after `server_connect` to verify `server.peername` against `_is_forbidden_peer_ip` right after TCP handshake completion, closing DNS-rebinding TOCTOU windows.
- **Modified** `config/default_settings/agents.yaml`:
  - Explicitly declared `github_allowed_repos: []` under `terrastella.egress` and `marvin.egress`.

### Isolated `brainsos-sandbox-net`, `litellm` Detachment & Minimal L7 `sandbox-gateway` (`HIGH-4`)
- **Modified** `docker-compose.yml`:
  - Defined `brainsos-sandbox-net` (`name: brainsos-sandbox-net`, `driver: bridge`, `internal: true`, bridge `br-sandbox`, subnet `172.28.8.0/24`, `ip_range: 172.28.8.128/25`).
- **Modified** `docker/compose/compose.control.yml`:
  - Detached `litellm` (`brainsos-control-litellm`) from `brainsos-internal-net`; `litellm` attaches strictly to `brainsos-control-net` (`172.28.2.10`) and `brainsos-ingress-net` (`172.28.0.11`, carrying `litellm`/`brainsos-litellm`/`brainsos-infra-litellm`/`brainsos-control-litellm` aliases).
  - Added `sandbox-gateway` (`caddy@sha256:5f5c8640aae01df9654968d946d8f1a56c497f1dd5c5cda4cf95ab7c14d58648`, `user: "65532:65532"`, `read_only: true`, `cap_drop: [ALL]`, `cap_add: [NET_BIND_SERVICE]`, `pids_limit: 128`, `security_opt: ["no-new-privileges:true"]`, zero env secrets, mounting `../../config/sandbox-gateway:/etc/caddy:ro`) bridging `brainsos-sandbox-net` (`172.28.8.10`, exposing `litellm` & `lens` aliases) and `brainsos-internal-net` (`172.28.1.14`, exposing `litellm` aliases only, preserving `lens` on `brainsos-internal-net`) to `brainsos-ingress-net` (`172.28.0.19`).
- **Created** `config/sandbox-gateway/Caddyfile`:
  - `:4000` allowlists strictly `POST /v1/chat/completions /v1/completions /v1/responses /v1/embeddings` and `GET /v1/models` -> `reverse_proxy {$LITELLM_UPSTREAM:litellm:4000}`, returning `403` for all other routes (LiteLLM admin, UI, SSO, `/key/*`, `/model/*`, `/user/*`, `/config`).
  - `:4318` allowlists strictly `POST /v1/traces /v1/logs` -> `reverse_proxy {$LENS_UPSTREAM:lens:4318}`, returning `403` for all trace-read/query routes.
- **Modified** `packages/brainsOS-runner/brainsos_runner/clients/docker.py` & `config/default_settings/runners.yaml`:
  - Switched `EphemeralDockerRunnerClient` default `network` to `"brainsos-sandbox-net"`, split `"--network", self.network` in `docker run` argv, included `sandbox-gateway` / `brainsos-litellm` in default `NO_PROXY`, and set `network: "brainsos-sandbox-net"` on all `ephemeral_docker` entries in `runners.yaml`.
- **Modified** `README.md`:
  - Aligned component table, Mermaid diagram, and L1–L7 architecture diagram with 9 isolated Docker bridge networks (`brainsos-sandbox-net`), `sandbox-gateway`, `litellm` detachment, and `BRAINSOS-LINKLOCAL`.

### `mitmweb:8081` Isolation, Config-File `web_password`, `AUTH_ALB_TRUSTED_CIDRS` & Multi-Bridge IMDS Guard (`HIGH-4`, `INFO-1`)
- **Created** `docker/egress/entrypoint.sh` & **Modified** `docker/egress/Dockerfile`, `config/egress/addons/theme_loader.py`:
  - Changed `brainsos-mitmweb.css` permissions in `docker/egress/Dockerfile` from `0o666` to `0o644` and installed `/usr/local/bin/brainsos-egress-entrypoint.sh`.
  - `entrypoint.sh` fails closed (`exit 1`) on startup when `TOOL_EGRESS_REQUIRE_PROXY_AUTH=true` and both `TOOL_EGRESS_PROXY_SECRET` and `TOOL_EGRESS_PROXY_TOKENS` are empty, writes `/run/mitmproxy/config.yaml` (`0600`) outside the shared `tool_egress_proxy_data` volume (hashing `web_password` with `argon2` when supported, JSON-escaped fallback otherwise), unsets `TOOL_EGRESS_WEB_PASSWORD`, and chains to `/usr/local/bin/docker-entrypoint.sh`.
  - `ThemeLoaderAddon.load()` loads `/run/mitmproxy/config.yaml` via `mitmproxy.optmanager.load_paths` when `web_password` is registered in `ctx.options`.
- **Modified** `docker/compose/compose.agents.yml`, `docker-compose.yml`, `config/caddy/Caddyfile`, `config/caddy/Caddyfile.appliance`:
  - Removed `--set web_password=...` from `tool-egress-proxy` `command:`, bound `--web-host ${TOOL_EGRESS_WEB_HOST:-172.28.0.18}`, attached `tool-egress-proxy:8082` to `brainsos-sandbox-net` (`172.28.8.2`), and attached `tool-egress-proxy` to `brainsos-ingress-net` (`172.28.0.18`, `gw_priority: 100`, alias `tool-egress-web`) in root `docker-compose.yml` (keeping `compose.agents.yml` detached from `brainsos-ingress-net`), routing Caddy `/efw/*` to `tool-egress-web:8081`.
  - Narrowed `AUTH_ALB_TRUSTED_CIDRS` defaults in `compose.ingress.yml`, `Caddyfile`, `Caddyfile.appliance`, and `.env.example` to `10.0.0.0/8 172.16.0.0/13 172.24.0.0/14 172.29.0.0/16 172.30.0.0/15 192.168.0.0/16` (excluding `172.28.0.0/16`).
- **Modified** `scripts/setup/imds-guard.sh`:
  - Removed obsolete `LITELLM_INTERNAL_IP:-172.28.1.11` fallback from `EGRESS_ALLOWED_IPS`.
  - Added `BRAINSOS-LINKLOCAL` chain (`ALLOWED_IMDS_IPS` -> `169.254.169.254/32 RETURN`, followed by `-d 169.254.0.0/16 -j DROP`) attached in `DOCKER-USER` after `#1 BRAINSOS-IMDS` and `#2 BRAINSOS-EGRESS` across all `brainsos-*-net` bridges (including `br-sandbox`) and `docker0`.
