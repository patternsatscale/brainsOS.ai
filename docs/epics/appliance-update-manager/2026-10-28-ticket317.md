## 1. Summary of Changes

This ticket establishes automated host-level and container-level log management, systemd journal vacuuming, and disk space retention policies across all brainsOS appliance deployments (AWS ARM64 Graviton AMIs, ASUS Ascent GX10 appliances, and local macOS workstations). This guarantees that long-running appliances will not exhaust disk storage or crash unexpectedly over months of continuous unattended operation.
