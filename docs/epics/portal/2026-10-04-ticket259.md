## 1. Executive Summary

This ticket integrates the Code-Server IDE (Operator Studio) and the Tool Egress Proxy (`mitmweb`) behind Authentik Forward-Auth, eliminating redundant secondary login prompts:
1. **Removed Legacy Caddy Basic Auth**: Stripped the hardcoded `basic_auth` block from `config/caddy/Caddyfile`, eliminating browser basic auth popups.
2. **Zero-Password IDE Authentication & Fixuid Bypass**: In `docker/editor/entrypoint.sh`, bypassed `fixuid` (which fails under `no-new-privileges:true`) to launch `/usr/bin/code-server` directly with `--auth none` behind Caddy `forward_auth`.
3. **Mitmweb Seamless Bearer Injection**: Removed invalid `web_base_path` flag (unsupported by mitmweb). Instead, configured Caddy `handle /efw/*` with `uri strip_prefix /efw` and automatic `Authorization: Bearer {$TOOL_EGRESS_WEB_PASSWORD}` upstream injection, completely eliminating secondary password prompts.
4. **Seamless Experience**: Operators authenticated through Authentik land directly in the IDE and egress flow console with zero secondary login prompts.
