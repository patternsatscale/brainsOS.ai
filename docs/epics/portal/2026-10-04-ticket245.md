## 1. Executive Summary

This ticket establishes the zero-trust identity layer for brainsOS, deploying an isolated **Authentik IdP** container stack, integrating Caddy with reverse-proxy `forward_auth`, and styling the authentication interface with a bespoke cybernetic theme matching the approved visual mockup ([docs/epics/portal/stitch/login.html](file:///Users/pats/Development/brainsOS.ai/docs/epics/portal/stitch/login.html)).

Key deliverables:
1. **Centralized SSO Topology**: Deployed `authentik-server`, `authentik-worker`, `authentik-db` (isolated Postgres 16), and `authentik-redis` on `brainsos-internal` and `brainsos-ingress`.
2. **Caddy Forward-Auth Snippet**: Configured `(authentik_forward_auth)` in `config/caddy/Caddyfile` using `/outpost.goauthentik.io/auth/caddy` to inject identity headers (`Remote-User`, `Remote-Email`, `X-Authentik-Username`, `X-Authentik-Groups`).
3. **Bespoke Cybernetic Theme**: Codified `config/authentik/themes/brainsos/login.html` and `theme.css` featuring operator username authentication, obsidian card styling, neon cyan glowing borders, and Passkey/FIDO2 triggers.
4. **Zero-Trust Guardrails**: Fully compliant with Rule 4 (zero docker socket mounts, unprivileged containers) and Rule 6 (Postgres database strictly isolated on internal networks).
