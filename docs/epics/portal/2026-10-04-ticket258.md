## 1. Executive Summary

This ticket configures SOGo Webmail & Groupware to accept Authentik Forward-Auth SSO credentials via Caddy reverse-proxy trusted headers, granting authenticated operators direct inbox access without secondary IMAP login prompts:
1. **SOGo Trusted Proxy Authentication**: Enabled `SOGoTrustProxyAuthentication = YES;` in `docker/sogo/sogo.conf`.
2. **Reverse-Proxy Header Injection**: Authentik standardizes on `X-Authentik-Username` (it does not emit `Remote-User` by default). Configured Caddy's `/mail*` and `/SOGo*` reverse-proxy blocks to pass `x-webobjects-remote-user: {header.X-Authentik-Username}` along with `Remote-User` and `X-Authentik-Username`.
3. **SOGo Nginx WebObjects Routing & Redirect Preservation**: Updated `docker/sogo/nginx.conf` to forward outer Caddy WebObjects headers (`x-webobjects-server-name`, `x-webobjects-server-url`, `x-webobjects-server-port`) and fallback to `$http_x_authentik_username` if `$http_x_webobjects_remote_user` is empty, ensuring SOGo redirects stay anchored to `https://osx.local.brainsos.ai` rather than leaking internal container hostnames.
4. **Seamless Inbox Entry**: Operators authenticated via Authentik land directly in the `operator@brainsos.ai` mailbox (`ng-app="SOGo.MailerUI"`).
5. **Daemon Port Parity**: Preserved direct loopback ports (`:10143`, `:10025`) for daemon and automated agent queue operations.
