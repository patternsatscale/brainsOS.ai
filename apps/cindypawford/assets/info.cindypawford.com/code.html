Patterns at Scale is an independent research initiative dedicated to the empiric investigation of autonomous agents operating under strict physical, computational, and electrical constraints.

We test autonomous perception, decision trees, and adversarial resilience directly in air-gapped sandboxes—unencumbered by corporate opacity or speculative monetization. Rather than treating compute as an unmetered cloud abstraction, our work grounds machine intelligence in real-world thermodynamics, sovereign edge execution, and open peer inspection.
